THE OTHER SIDE

An SEL Choose-Your-Own-Adventure

To the student playing this game:

You are about to step into the shoes of Alex — a 7th grader who has been hurting other students at school. This game is NOT about celebrating bullying. It is about understanding why someone might act that way — and discovering that there is always another path.

Your choices matter. Pay attention to how Alex feels underneath the behavior.

⚠ NOTE FOR EDUCATORS: This game is designed for use with a trained school counselor or psychologist. Please debrief with students after play. Discussion questions are provided at the end.
